For several years I've worked alongside researchers and other presenters here in Japan, helping them make their work understood by people outside their field. These are notes from that room: small things I've watched happen, and what they've taught me about how ideas travel.

The world your students are entering

  • 3× — Japanese students studying abroad more than tripled in the decade before the pandemic (about 36,000 to 115,000).
  • 27% — of scientific papers across OECD countries now carry a co-author abroad, up from 22% a decade earlier.
  • ~93% — of the world's indexed research is published in English.

Sources: MEXT/JASSO; OECD; Scopus.
