How AI Empowers Coaching

These days, they throw everything at me as a communications coach:

  • medicine
  • biology
  • sociology
  • engineering
  • economics
  • psychology
  • linguistics

— the whole college catalog!

I've coached communication for a long time, but even two years ago, working across this range of disciplines was beyond my — or anyone's — reach.

But now, before a session, AI helps me work through any paper. It makes clear the scientific landscape, what problems the researchers are attacking, their findings, and which terms are critical. A paper that was first a maze of technical language opens up.

And that helps me help the researcher communicate to their audience. We can also explore and practice likely Q&A topics or conversations with a potential collaborator or even a financial supporter.

One medical researcher brought his complex work on mechanical ventilation in COVID treatment. AI quickly helped me understand his point that inflating the lungs can also damage them. In our session, we worked on explaining why devices that inflate lungs more gently could matter to an audience that included potential supporters. He supplied the scientific judgment; I helped him find a path through the explanation that someone outside the field could follow.

AI, as we know, can make errors or miss a distinction. But then the researcher can tell me where my understanding is wrong. And while we talk, I can hear where their explanation may lose a listener.

This is all very exciting to me. I can meet a researcher in almost any discipline with enough understanding to begin a useful interaction. From there, we do the work together: finding what matters, deciding what the evidence supports, and making it possible for any audience to understand.
